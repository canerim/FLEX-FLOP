\title{\vspace{-2.2em}What choosing the exit map costs: end-to-end encoder and decoder runtime\\\large DCVC-UF on the 53 CTC intra frames, NoGAN-MS and ELIC on Kodak-24 and CLIC-41}\author{FLEX project}\date{\today}
\begin{document}\maketitle\vspace{-1.8em}
\paragraph{Summary.} On 1080p frames a plain DCVC-UF encode (analysis, priors and rANS coding) takes 447\,ms and a plain decode 605\,ms. Guaranteeing $\le$0.1\,dB per frame with the fast oracle raises the encoder to 2.25$\times$ (the extra work equals 1.4 released synthesis passes), and delivers 29.3\,\% decoder MAC saving; the decoder then runs in 86\,\% of the released decoder's time. The same map as first shipped cost 6.78$\times$. A decoder-side router leaves the encoder untouched only without the guarantee (27.4\,\%, 120 of 265 frame-qp over budget, worst 0.278\,dB); with it, the router needs the table (2.60$\times$) or 10 routed decodes (8.95$\times$). Its advantage is side information (8 bits instead of 57 bits per frame, 0.066\,\% of the stream on average), not encoder time. For the in-place gated image decoders the oracle is an offline search of 19--23 table decodes plus 14 search decodes per image.

\section*{1\quad Setup}
\textbf{Data.} DCVC-UF: the first intra frame of all 53 sequences in DCVC's own CTC configuration (UVG 7, MCL-JCV 30, HEVC B 5, C 4, D 4, E 3), four resolutions from 416$\times$240 to 1920$\times$1080, qp 0, 16, 32, 48, 63 (265 frame-qp). Frames are padded to whole 256-px tiles; rates are real rANS bytes over the true pixel count. Image decoders: Kodak-24 (768$\times$512) and CLIC-41 (professional validation, $\approx$2048$\times$1365).
\textbf{Models.} DCVC-UF RECIPE512 e15 ladder (6 exits, split depth 2, 256-px tiles, grid seam repair), router head ce\_soft (148k parameters); NoGAN-MS q3 60k (9 gated blocks, 64-px cells); ELIC N=192 with MAC-priced stand-ins (11 gated stages, 256-px cells).
\textbf{Timing.} NVIDIA RTX A6000, PyTorch, TF32 off, CUDA-synchronised wall clock per stage; the arithmetic coder is the released C++ rANS on the CPU. All stages of one frame are timed back to back, so contention on the shared card affects every regime alike; tables report medians. Timing jitter measured on one 1080p frame repeated 10 times: coefficient of variation 6--51\,\% per stage (Fig.~S1). The DCVC-UF release ships its coder as a CUDA extension we do not build, so the plain pipeline is re-assembled from the model's own modules; the decoder's entropy stage decodes the same symbols in the coder's LIFO order, which is the same work as the sequential decoder.
\textbf{Budget.} Per-frame loss $\le$0.1\,dB against the released decoder's full-frame reconstruction of the same latent (ratio convention); savings are decoder MAC savings from the measured cost table.

\paragraph{Decoder.} At 1080p the plain decoder spends 21\,ms in rANS decoding, 181\,ms in the hyper-decoder and spatial prior, and 399\,ms in synthesis; only synthesis is gated. Routed synthesis takes 333\,ms with the oracle map (17\,\% less time for 29\,\% fewer MACs), so the whole decoder runs in 86\,\% of the released time. At 416$\times$240 the tiled routed path is slower than the released decoder (39 against 22\,ms of synthesis): the frame is padded to 512$\times$256 and split into two 256-px tiles, and the per-tile overhead exceeds the MACs saved. The routed decoder is faster end to end from 1280$\times$720 upward (Table~4); below that, per-tile overhead exceeds the MACs saved.

\paragraph{Budget.} Every guaranteed regime keeps every feasible frame at or under 0.1\,dB. The frame-qp listed over budget under R1, R2 (1) and R4, R5 (2) are frames whose deepest-exit tiling floor already exceeds the budget; no map can meet it and the deepest map is sent. Without the per-frame search (router, fixed $\beta$) 120 of 265 frame-qp exceed the budget, worst 0.278\,dB.

\paragraph{Reading.} In DCVC-UF the tiles are independent, so one tapped pass through the per-tile trunk gives every exit's distortion and the encoder's selection is cheap. In the image decoders a block runs wherever any cell within its receptive field asks for it, so the delivered loss and cost of a map are only known by decoding it; the oracle there is a measurement procedure, and its cost is dominated by the per-configuration decodes and by the halo-aware MAC accounting, which is bookkeeping and can be vectorised.
