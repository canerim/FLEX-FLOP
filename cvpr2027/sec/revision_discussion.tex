\section{Discussion}

Spatial placement matters most at tight quality targets. Near the cheapest
exit's ceiling, blind mixing approaches learned routing; validation should
choose when the added decision cost pays for itself.

Source calibration reveals allocation potential, but fixed-control replay
does not yet resolve its gain over blind mixing. A content-grouped unseen
cohort and paired native wall-clock tests must include prediction, tile
movement, entropy and fallback. Intervals here vary images at fixed weights,
not training seeds. Intra YCbCr/RGB results do not establish perceptual
equivalence or performance on the video path.

\section{Conclusion}
RegLIC makes synthesis depth a spatial decision inside DCVC-UF.
Aligned exits and a shrinking tile batch reuse a common representation
while omitting later blocks. Shallower depth supplies most measured arithmetic
saving; learned placement adds a smaller, budget-dependent increment.
Matched training and complete execution measurements will decide when
independent-depth experts justify losing shared computation.
