# Heading structure taken from these papers

| Paper | Venue | Structure we borrow |
|---|---|---|
| ClassSR (Kong et al.) | CVPR 2021 | Related work incl. "Region-aware image restoration"; Method = Observation → Overview → modules → losses → training → discussion; ablations as named paragraphs |
| APE, Adaptive Patch Exiting (Wang et al.) | ECCV 2022 | Multi-exit training → capacity estimation → joint training; "Comparison with ClassSR" |
| ARM, Any-Time SR (Chen et al.) | ECCV 2022 | Preliminary → supernet training → supernet inference |
| PCSR, Pixel-Level Classification SR (Jeong et al.) | ECCV 2024 | Preliminary → architecture → training → inference; "Limitation and Future Works" |
| SMSR, Sparsity in Image SR (Wang et al.) | CVPR 2021 | A standalone observation section before the method |
| SkipNet (Wang et al.) | ECCV 2018 | Gating design → policy learning; skipping-behaviour analysis |
| BlockDrop (Wu et al.) | CVPR 2018 | Pretrained ResNet → policy network → training; qualitative policies |
| SACT, Spatially Adaptive Computation Time (Figurnov et al.) | CVPR 2017 | Residual net → ACT → spatial ACT |
| MSDNet (Huang et al.) | ICLR 2018 | Problem setup; anytime vs budgeted evaluation |
| A-ViT (Yin et al.) | CVPR 2022 | Analysis subsection with token-depth visualisation |
| DynamicViT (Rao et al.) | NeurIPS 2021 | Overview → prediction modules → end-to-end optimisation → training and inference |
| EVC (Wang et al.) | ICLR 2023 | Codec-side: comparison with SOTA → method components → ablations; long appendix |
| Shallow decoders (Yang & Mandt) | ICCV 2023 | "The case for a shallow decoder" motivation subsection; decoder complexity framing |
| SlimCAE (Yang et al.) | CVPR 2021 | Related work "Efficiency"; RD + efficiency + scalable bitstreams sections |
| DCVC-RT (Jia et al.) | CVPR 2025 | "Rethink the complexity problem" section; complexity analysis; per-module complexity in supp |
