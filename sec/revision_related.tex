\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Making learned decoding cheaper.}
Shallow synthesis transforms reduce decoder computation globally;
stronger analysis or iterative encoding can compensate for some of the
lost reconstruction capacity~\cite{yang2023shallow}. DCVC-RT instead
emphasises operational costs such as memory traffic and function
calls~\cite{jia2025dcvcrt}, while DCVC-UF increases video throughput with
chunk-based coding and streamlined entropy interaction~\cite{li2026dcvcuf}.
We modify only DCVC-UF's intra synthesis transform. These approaches
motivate two controls in our study: a fixed shallow decoder isolates the
value of adaptation, and full execution measurements are required before
arithmetic reduction can be called acceleration.

\paragraph{Choosing a network or choosing an exit.}
ClassSR learns to assign super-resolution patches to networks of different
capacity~\cite{kong2021classsr}. APE instead lets patches leave a multi-exit
network according to predicted incremental reconstruction
benefit~\cite{wang2022ape}; it already shares the reconstruction tail across
exits. AdaRevD predicts patch difficulty to choose a deblurring
sub-decoder~\cite{mao2024adarevd}; RBQE terminates compressed-image
enhancement early~\cite{xing2020rbqe}. Swift introduces compute-dependent
early exits in a layered neural video decoder, with different output
resolutions~\cite{dasari2022swift}. FLEX-UF addresses the codec-specific case within
DCVC-UF synthesis at fixed output resolution: one entropy-decoded latent
feeds conditional feature
tiles, exit-specific adapters align those features, and reconstruction
occurs after full-frame assembly. Our proposed independent-depth bank
follows the ClassSR allocation principle, but selecting a codec also
selects an analysis transform and entropy model.

\paragraph{Adaptive capacity inside a codec.}
Slimmable compressive autoencoders jointly optimise rate and distortion
at multiple network widths~\cite{yang2021slimcae}. AdaNIC is the closest
spatial-capacity precedent: blockwise spatially adaptive operators and a
router vary transform width within an image~\cite{tao2023adanic}.
For video, cgSlimDecoder learns channel allocations across decoder
modules under complexity constraints~\cite{hu2023cgslim}. FLEX-UF varies
\emph{depth} through nested, constant-width synthesis blocks and executes
later stages on a shrinking tile batch. Its evaluation asks an additional
question: how much of the saving requires content-aware placement after
blind mixtures have already reduced the average depth?

\paragraph{Spatial selection among complete codecs.}
Blard et al. train content-specialised codecs of the same architecture,
search their regional rate--distortion costs, and signal a mode
map~\cite{blard2026spatial}. Their method also processes connected regions
of one mode jointly. Our region-merging diagnostic therefore tests an
existing strategy in DCVC-UF, rather than introducing it. The planned bank
varies synthesis depth and investigates selection before candidate encoding;
the evaluated shared-exit path instead retains one representation. These
distinctions separate specialisation, capacity and representation reuse,
which cannot be inferred from a comparison of model sizes alone.

\paragraph{Preserving context across blocks.}
Kim et al. derive overlap and boundary rules that preserve full-image
CNN reconstruction~\cite{kim2026blockwise}. FLEX-UF instead accepts
context loss in its zero-halo suffix and learns a common repair/head.
Its tiled all-deep output must therefore be evaluated separately from
full-frame synthesis.
