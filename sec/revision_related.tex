\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Making learned decoding cheaper.}
Shallow synthesis reduces decoder computation, with stronger analysis
or iterative encoding compensating for lost capacity~\cite{yang2023shallow}.
DCVC-RT emphasises memory traffic and function calls~\cite{jia2025dcvcrt};
DCVC-UF combines chunk-based video coding with streamlined entropy
interaction~\cite{li2026dcvcuf}. We modify its intra synthesis transform.
A fixed shallow decoder controls for adaptation, while complete execution
measurements distinguish arithmetic reduction from acceleration.

\paragraph{Choosing a network or choosing an exit.}
ClassSR learns to assign super-resolution patches to networks of different
capacity~\cite{kong2021classsr}. APE instead lets patches leave a multi-exit
network according to predicted incremental reconstruction
benefit~\cite{wang2022ape}; it already shares the reconstruction tail across
exits. AdaRevD predicts patch difficulty to choose a deblurring
sub-decoder~\cite{mao2024adarevd}; RBQE terminates compressed-image
enhancement early~\cite{xing2020rbqe}. Swift introduces compute-dependent
early exits in a layered neural video decoder, with different output
resolutions~\cite{dasari2022swift}. RegLIC retains one entropy-decoded latent
and fixed output resolution: adapters align conditional feature tiles
before full-frame reconstruction. The proposed independent-depth bank
also selects the analysis transform and entropy model.

\paragraph{Adaptive capacity inside a codec.}
Slimmable compressive autoencoders jointly optimise rate and distortion
at multiple network widths~\cite{yang2021slimcae}. ELFIC allocates decoding
complexity per instance~\cite{zhang2023elfic}; ABC adapts backbone and
autoregressive structure to content and computational budgets~\cite{zhang2025abc}.
AdaNIC varies transform width spatially through blockwise operators
and a router~\cite{tao2023adanic}.
For video, cgSlimDecoder allocates decoder channels under complexity
constraints~\cite{hu2023cgslim}. MixCompress routes among different-depth
experts using only the rate embedding, allowing assignments to be
cached independently of image content~\cite{ta2026mixcompress}. RegLIC varies
\emph{depth} through nested, constant-width synthesis blocks and executes
later stages on a shrinking tile batch. We measure the spatial placement
premium over blind mixtures that already reduce average depth.

\paragraph{Spatial selection among complete codecs.}
Blard et al. train content-specialised codecs of the same architecture,
search their regional rate--distortion costs, and signal a mode
map~\cite{blard2026spatial}. They also process connected same-mode regions
jointly; our merging diagnostic tests this existing strategy in DCVC-UF.
The planned bank varies synthesis depth and investigates selection before
candidate encoding. Shared exit retains one representation, separating
representation reuse from independent-expert specialisation.

\paragraph{Preserving context across blocks.}
Kim et al. derive overlap and boundary rules that preserve full-image
CNN reconstruction~\cite{kim2026blockwise}. RegLIC instead accepts
context loss in its zero-halo suffix and learns a common repair/head.
Its tiled all-deep output must therefore be evaluated separately from
full-frame synthesis.
