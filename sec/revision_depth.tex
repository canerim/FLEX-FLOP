\section{A Controlled Six-Depth Study}
\label{sec:depth}

Shared early exit trades representation reuse against specialised capacity.
Our next study compares independently trained DCVC-UF codecs retaining
2/4/6/8/10/12 synthesis blocks. Their encoder and entropy parameters also
learn, so their latents are not interchangeable exits of the current model.
\Cref{tab:depth} reports exact capacity and experimental status; completed
matched RD and latency results are pending.

Each variant preserves the opening upsampler and output head. D2 removes
73.0\% of D12's synthesis parameters but only 24.6\% of complete-codec
parameters, because analysis and entropy capacity remain. A same-recipe
D12 trained from scratch must complement the released anchor to isolate
depth from training provenance. Likewise, attributing a gap between this
bank and the older e15 fine-tuning run specifically to weight sharing
requires matched data, initialisation and optimisation controls.

\paragraph{Training and evaluation.}
The independent runs retain the pinned official 105-epoch
recipe~\cite{microsoft2026training}, all 384,795 Open Images train\_0/1/2
images, batch size 16 and FP32. The supplement records the schedule and
execution settings. Four validation crops monitor health; final comparison
requires common achieved RD, complete coded bytes and paired timing.
Equal QP is not equal rate across codecs.

\paragraph{Later encoder-side selection.}
The proposed MLP in \cref{fig:depthsystem} selects an expert before patch
encoding and transmits its identity. Controls are full-frame D12,
patchwise D12, a validation-selected fixed expert and blind mixtures.
Leave-one-expert-out tests measure patch-specific value. Training,
calibration and test images stay separate; timing includes grouping,
entropy resets, signalling and fallback. The supplement's dashed runtime
scenarios expose assumed fixed costs without inventing quality or speed
measurements for unfinished models.
